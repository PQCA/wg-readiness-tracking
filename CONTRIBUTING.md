Contributions are welcome from anybody. Please follow these guidelines:

1. The information must be public.
2. Please link to details supporting the compatibility information provided.
3. Linked information should be technical in nature (technical documentation, link to a pull request, ...). Please do not link to press releases or marketing materials.
4. PRs that change readiness details will be discussed and committed, if approved, during WG meetings. Reviews prior to meetings are strongly encouraged.
5. If you have questions, please ask them on the mailing list - https://lists.pqca.org/g/wg-readiness-tracking.
